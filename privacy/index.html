Privacy Policy

Turtle Cleaning Services LLC · Last updated September 21, 2026

This page covers this contact site, the Turtle Cleaning Services Facebook Page, and the @turtle_cleaning_services Instagram account.

This site

This site is a small set of static pages: a link to WhatsApp, a phone number, and a cleaning estimate form. The estimate form sends what you type straight to us; we do not store it anywhere else, sell it, or share it with anyone outside Turtle Cleaning Services. The rest of the site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic server logs like any web host; we do not access or use those logs.

Our Facebook and Instagram accounts

We use Meta's publishing tools only to post our own photos, reels and captions to our own Page and Instagram account. This does not collect, read or share visitor data, and we do not run ads through it.

If you message us

If you contact us on WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, by phone, or through the estimate form, we use what you send only to answer you and, if you hire us, to schedule and complete the work. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram belong to Meta and have their own privacy policies, linked below.
