Privacy Policy

Turtle Cleaning Services LLC · Last updated October 14, 2026

This page covers this contact site, the Turtle Cleaning Services Facebook Page, and the @turtle_cleaning_services Instagram account.

This site

This site is a small set of static pages: a link to WhatsApp, a phone number, and a cleaning estimate form. The estimate form sends what you type straight to us: it is saved in a spreadsheet and an email alert in our own Google account, and nowhere else. We do not sell it or share it with anyone outside Turtle Cleaning Services. The estimate form page uses the Meta pixel, described under “Ads and measurement”; the other pages set no cookies and run no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic server logs like any web host; we do not access or use those logs.

Our Facebook and Instagram accounts

We use Meta's publishing tools only to post our own photos, reels and captions to our own Page and Instagram account. This does not collect, read or share visitor data. Our ads are described in the next section.

Ads and measurement

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram. The estimate form page uses the Meta pixel, a small piece of code from Meta that uses cookies and similar technologies to collect or receive information from this site and elsewhere on the internet. Meta and we use that information to measure how our ads perform and to target and deliver ads. On this site the pixel reports that the page was opened and that a request was sent. It does not read or send what you type in the form.

You can opt out of the collection and use of information for ad targeting at aboutads.info/choices and youronlinechoices.eu, and in your Facebook ad settings.

If you message us

If you contact us on WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, by phone, or through the estimate form, we use what you send only to answer you and, if you hire us, to schedule and complete the work. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram belong to Meta and have their own privacy policies, linked below.
